Wholesale

Stock sync with a marketplace

Why a once-a-day stock export was costing the client its rating — and how that is fixed in a week.

Industry
wholesale
Timeline
11 days
Budget
130 000 ₽
Stack
Node.js, REST API, Docker

The problem

Stock levels were uploaded to the marketplace as a file once a day. An item sold offline in the morning stayed on sale until the next day: orders had to be cancelled, the shop’s rating dropped, and the sales team kept a parallel spreadsheet of “what we actually have”.

What I did

  1. A layer between the inventory system and the marketplace: it listens for stock changes and sends them in batches.
  2. Sync every five minutes, and immediately for large changes.
  3. Retries with an increasing back-off: a temporary failure on the marketplace side no longer breaks the exchange.
  4. Notifications to the team’s Telegram channel: what went through, what didn’t and why.
  5. An operation log that shows the history of every item.

How it works

The service runs in Docker on the client’s server and keeps its state in a queue so changes survive a restart. Logs are rotated so the disk never fills up.

The result

  • 5 minutesdelay instead of a full day
  • −90%fewer cancelled orders
  • 0manual uploads per day
