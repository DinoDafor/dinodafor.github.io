Country houses

Landing page with a calculator and leads in the CRM

How to remove the first fifteen minutes of obvious questions from every sales call.

Industry
construction
Timeline
7 days
Budget
$860
Stack
HTML, CSS, vanilla JS, amoCRM API

The problem

Ads led to a page with no structure. Visitors could not tell the ballpark price and left, and those who did enquire wrote a single word: “interested”. The sales manager spent the first minutes of every call asking about floor area, storeys and budget.

What I did

  1. Built the page around the one question a client has: how much will a house cost for my requirements.
  2. A calculator: floor area, storeys, material, foundation — it shows a price range and a timeline.
  3. A short four-question quiz instead of a ten-field form.
  4. Each enquiry creates a deal in amoCRM with the client’s answers attached and pings the manager on Telegram.
  5. Analytics with goals: it shows which quiz step loses people.

How it works

The page is static, with no CMS: it loads instantly and needs no maintenance. Enquiries go straight to the CRM, and the API key lives on a small proxy server rather than in the page code.

The result

  • ×2,4enquiry conversion rate
  • 2 minutesaverage response time
  • 7 daysfrom mock-up to live ads
